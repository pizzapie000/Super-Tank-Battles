factory_alarm.wav
-----------------
Adapted from "Alarm" by EZduzziteh (July 11, 2020).
Source page: https://opengameart.org/content/alarm-1
Original download: https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/alarm_2.ogg
License: CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain dedication).
License details: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

CC0 permits copying, modification, and redistribution, including commercial
use, without requiring attribution. Author credit is retained as a courtesy.

Game adaptation: lowered pitch and slowed playback to 80% of the original,
converted to mono 44.1 kHz PCM WAV, filtered the low rumble and sharp high
frequencies, added gentle attack/release fades, adjusted the phrase level,
and added 0.8 seconds of silence between repetitions for a less tiring loop.
The source is not loop-enabled globally; the factory uses a looping copy and
other story events play the same softened alarm once.


tank_movement.wav
-----------------
Adapted from "engine4_loop.wav" by ReadeOnly (April 29, 2013).
Source page: https://freesound.org/people/ReadeOnly/sounds/186934/
Public high-quality preview: https://cdn.freesound.org/previews/186/186934_545448-hq.mp3
License: CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain dedication).
License details: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

CC0 permits copying, modification, and redistribution, including commercial
use, without requiring attribution. Author credit is retained as a courtesy.

Game adaptation: selected an early 5.6-second section to avoid a click later
in the source, filtered below 60 Hz and above 1400 Hz, converted to mono
44.1 kHz PCM WAV, crossfaded the loop boundary over 0.3 seconds, and adjusted
the peak level. The resulting seamless loop is 5.3 seconds long. Battles play
one shared movement loop at up to -24 dB, fading with actual tank movement.
